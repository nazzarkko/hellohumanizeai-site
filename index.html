How we work

Every conversation is one-to-one. We write to editors personally about a specific site and a specific idea — no mailing lists, no mass campaigns.

  1. We reach out about one concrete placement on your site.
  2. You share your price, guidelines and timing.
  3. We send the content or the mention for your approval.
  4. You publish, we pay. Not interested? Tell us once and we will not write again.
