Anti-spam policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Our team does not send, and does not allow anyone to send on its behalf, unsolicited bulk email.

How we contact people

  • We contact website owners and editors only individually, one person at a time, about specific content partnership proposals.
  • We never buy, rent or share email lists.
  • Every message clearly identifies the sender and how to reach us.

Opting out

Anyone who asks not to be contacted is removed permanently and is not contacted again by any member of our team.

Third parties

Affiliates and third parties are not permitted to advertise this domain or our services by email.

Report abuse

If you believe you received an unsolicited message from us, please write to evelina.collab@hellohumanizeai.com and we will act on it promptly.
